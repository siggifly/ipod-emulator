# Licensing

| what | licence |
|---|---|
| all code (`tools/`, the crates, the recipes) | **GPL-3.0-or-later** — `LICENSE` |
| everything under `research/` | **CC BY-SA 4.0** — `research/LICENSE` |

## Why two

They are different kinds of work. The code is a program; the research is documentation, and CC BY-SA
is what documentation of this kind is normally under, so people can quote it, translate it and build
on it as long as they credit and share alike.

## Why GPL rather than something permissive

So that nobody can take this, close it, and sell a product built on it without giving anything back.
GPL does not prevent commercial use and is not intended to — it requires that source travels with
the binary, which makes closing it impossible.

It is also the right neighbourhood. Rockbox is GPL, and Rockbox is where most of the register
semantics in this project came from. Nothing here is derived from Rockbox source, so there is no
obligation; licensing compatibly with the work you learned from is simply good manners.

## What is not covered, and never will be

Apple's firmware, NOR dumps, IPSW files and disk images are not distributed with this project and
are not licensed by it. They belong to Apple. You supply your own, from a device you own.
